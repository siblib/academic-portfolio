Why APA Expertise Matters

Eliminate desk rejection before reviewers even read your findings

Journal editors and dissertation committees review hundreds of submissions each term. When citations are mangled, headings inconsistent, or reference formats outdated, reviewers form an immediate negative impression of the study's methodological rigor.

With deep specialization in APA 7th edition standards, I transform rough manuscripts into publication-ready documents that command reviewer respect. In APA 7, student papers omit running heads while professional manuscripts require them; reference lists require DOIs as URLs and accommodate up to 20 authors. Precision in these details signals scholarly authority.

FAQ

Questions researchers ask before hiring an editor

Transparent answers on ethics, turnaround schedules, revision guarantees, and confidentiality.

Do you write or rewrite content for clients?+

No undisclosed authorship. Drafting support is collaborative and transparent: you set the intellectual direction, provide the research data and source material, and review and approve every passage before it enters your manuscript. I strictly decline projects where institutional policy prohibits such assistance, and I never engage in undisclosed ghostwriting. You retain full authorship and academic ownership.

What is your turnaround time, and can you handle urgent deadlines?+

Standard turnaround is 5 business days for manuscripts up to 15,000 words. For tight conference or journal submission deadlines, Express 48-business-hour turnaround is available for manuscripts up to 10,000 words. The timeline clock starts once complete, final files are received.

How does the 14-day revision policy work?+

You have 14 calendar days following delivery to review the work and submit up to two rounds of minor revisions tied to the original scope (such as formatting adjustments, style preferences, or editorial oversights). Revisions are returned within 2 business days.

How do you protect my research confidentiality and unpublished findings?+

Your research, data, and draft manuscripts are treated with strict confidentiality. Files are stored in private encrypted storage, accessible solely by me, and are permanently purged 30 days after project completion. A formal non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is available upon request prior to file upload.

What file formats do you accept, and what will I receive back?+

Please submit editable Microsoft Word (.docx) files so that Microsoft Word Track Changes can record all modifications. PDFs are accepted as visual reference copies only. Upon delivery, you receive both a tracked-changes .docx showing every edit and a clean, submission-ready .docx, plus a formatted PDF.

How does quoting and payment work?+

Formatting is quoted per page or reference count; line editing is quoted per word based on an initial sample assessment. You receive a transparent, fixed-price quote upfront with zero hidden fees. Once quoted, payments are processed securely via Stripe within your Client Portal.
